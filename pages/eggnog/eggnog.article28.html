Signs and symptoms of Wretched Unwellness -- October 17, 2026

♪Hideout - Deadlock OST (Composer Unknown)♪

Wretched Unwellness is a state of mental decay that erodes the bonding of one's soul to their body. When a person begins there descent into Wretched Unwellness, they display symptoms that dangerously reinforce their condition. Like a virus that hijacks the immune system, Wretched Unwellness abuses the host's ability to rationalize as a means to justify their own delusions. Below are some of the most common signs and symptoms of Wretched Unwellness.

-- Irritability --
The Unwell becomes irrationally upset by minor transgressions, even taking them as personal insults.

-- Paranoia --
The Unwell connects disparate events and details to their personal insecurities. This ranges from small details (a glance from a stranger appears to the Unwell as a scornful glare) to elaborate conspiracies (the Unwell believes their mother is plotting to poison them).

-- Reduced Vocabulary --
The Unwell shows a reduced vocabulary relative to their baseline. They find it difficult to express themselves with written and spoken word.

-- Egotism --
The Unwell sees themselves as more important than they are. They overestimate their own intelligence and ability while demeaning others. When another person makes a mistake, the Wretchedly Unwell considers the mistake as proof of both the other's incompetence and their own superiority. They often suffer from delusions of grandeur.

-- Tunnel Vision --
The Unwell is unable to see what is clear to others. They have trouble considering other perspectives.

-- Thought Validity --
The Unwell struggles to weigh the value of individual thoughts. Each thought that enters their brain seems equally valid, muddying their judgment.
